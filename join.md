## Open Positions

We do not have open lab positions at this time.

If you are interested in joining HEAL Lab in the future, we encourage you to apply to York University's graduate programs during the regular application period. Prospective **MSc and PhD** students with backgrounds in computer science, health informatics, data science, or related fields are welcome to mention HEAL Lab in their application or reach out once admitted.

Visit the [Faculty of Health](https://health.yorku.ca/) website for program details, admission requirements, and deadlines.

### What we look for

- Strong programming skills (Python, R, or similar)
- Interest in health policy, clinical informatics, or NLP
- Experience with data analysis, machine learning, or simulation (preferred)
