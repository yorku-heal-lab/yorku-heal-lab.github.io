## Open Positions

We are actively recruiting **graduate students** (MSc and PhD) with backgrounds in computer science, health informatics, data science, or related fields.

### What we look for

- Strong programming skills (Python, R, or similar)
- Interest in health policy, clinical informatics, or NLP
- Experience with data analysis, machine learning, or simulation (preferred)

### How to apply

1. Review our [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page and recent [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
2. Send a CV and brief statement of interest to [{{ site.data.lab.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.lab.contact.email }}).
3. Include any relevant code samples, transcripts, or writing samples.

## Collaborations

We welcome collaborations with researchers, healthcare organizations, and policy partners. For partnership inquiries, contact the lab at [{{ site.data.lab.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.lab.contact.email }}).

## York University Graduate Programs

Prospective students may apply through York University's graduate programs in Health Policy & Management and related fields. Visit the [Faculty of Health](https://health.yorku.ca/) website for admission requirements and deadlines.
